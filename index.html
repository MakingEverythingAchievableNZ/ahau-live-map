Why are there so many pieces here?

Think of the Āhau app as one screen on your phone. Behind it, dozens of tiny building blocks (called plugins/packages) work together. Every time you open a family tree or save a photo, your request passes through a chain of these pieces. Each card below is one of those pieces.

You'll never see any of this. When we ship, everything gets bundled ("compiled") into one single app you download and open, like Pātaka or Āhau desktop. The puzzle pieces only matter behind the scenes while we're building. Once it's out the door, it's just one clean, ready-to-use app.

This tool lets a full set of related records — a person's profile, their place in a family tree, their stories, and any photos or files attached to them — be gathered up and either exported as a portable backup, or copied wholesale into a different whānau or community group. It understands which records depend on which others (for example, a story needs its author's profile to exist first), so a copy or import happens in the right order and nothing ends up broken or missing its connections.

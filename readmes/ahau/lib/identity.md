This isn't code — it's a working document where the team was thinking through an idea called "digital identity": giving a person, a marae, an iwi, or a hapū a verifiable digital credential, similar in spirit to a digital ID card or membership card, that other people and organisations could check and trust.

The idea was that individuals and groups (whanau, hapū, iwi, even government agencies or banks) could issue and recognise credentials for one another — for example, an iwi vouching that someone is a registered member, or a hapū recognising someone as a kaumātua — building trust through mutual recognition rather than a single central authority deciding who counts.

It's full of unanswered questions and technical options the team was still weighing, and the work was paused before any of it was built.

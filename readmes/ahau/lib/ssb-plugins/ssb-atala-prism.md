This was a working prototype of the broader "digital credentials" idea the team was designing: it let a trusted person in a community — a kaitiaki — issue someone a verifiable digital credential, like proof of membership, and let that person later present it elsewhere to prove who they are or what group they belong to.

It relied on an outside identity service ("Atala PRISM") to do the actual issuing and verifying, and it's paused with a handful of known bugs still open — the connection-confirmation step didn't always work reliably.

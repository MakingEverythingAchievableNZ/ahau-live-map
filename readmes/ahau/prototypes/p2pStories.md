This was an early experiment testing a completely different technical approach: a phone app for recording and sharing short audio messages peer-to-peer, built on a different underlying toolkit than the one Āhau ended up using. It was a collaboration with a couple of outside projects working on similar peer-to-peer communication tools, aimed at seeing whether the idea would work well on low-end Android phones over local wifi or the internet. The approach wasn't carried forward into the main system.

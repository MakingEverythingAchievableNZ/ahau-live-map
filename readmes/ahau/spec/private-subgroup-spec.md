This document explains how a smaller, more private group can exist nested inside a larger community group — for example, a close family circle within a wider hapū or community. A subgroup gets its own separate encryption, so its members can share things privately even from other members of the parent group, while still being required to belong to that parent group in the first place.

It also describes an optional feature: a subgroup can have its own private "postbox" address, so people who aren't members yet can still send something to it directly — useful for someone applying to join.

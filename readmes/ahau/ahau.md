This is the main Āhau app itself — the one whanau actually open on their phone or computer to record whakapapa (family trees) along with the stories, photos, and knowledge that go with it. It works peer-to-peer, meaning it doesn't need the internet or a central company server to run: your data is held by you and the people you've connected with, and it's encrypted so only the people you choose can read it.

This one codebase is used to build both the desktop version and the mobile version of the app, sharing most of the same screens and features between them. For more, see ahau.io.

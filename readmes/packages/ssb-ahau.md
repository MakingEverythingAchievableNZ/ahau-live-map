This is the piece that assembles everything the main Āhau app needs into one working whole — it wires together the question-answering layer and every record-keeper it needs to talk to, including profiles, family trees, and stories.

It also handles a specific privacy feature: whenever someone creates or joins a private whanau or community group, this automatically creates a separate, private copy of their profile just for that group — so what they share with one group doesn't automatically carry over to another.

Pātaka is meant to run continuously on a machine with a fixed, always-reachable address, rather than on someone's personal phone or laptop. Its job is to hold and pass along encrypted copies of people's information and files so everyone can stay in sync, even when the person who created something is offline.

Importantly, Pātaka can't actually read any of the private information it stores and forwards — it only ever handles the encrypted version. It's a relay and backup point, not a place where anyone's private records are exposed.
